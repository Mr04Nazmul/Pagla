# Bangladesh Village: high-fidelity asset backlog

The procedural village generator produces low-detail stand-ins; these are the planned replacements required for realistic graphics.

| Category | Bangladesh-specific asset requirements | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terrain | brown muddy lane, wheel ruts, puddles, paddy banks | photos, PBR ground textures and actual landscape heightmap |
| Homes | corrugated tin rooftops, bamboo walls, bricks, verandas | original modular meshes based on village reference photos |
| Vegetation | rice paddy plants, banana, coconut, mango, jackfruit and date palms | licensed 3D foliage, wind and LOD setup |
| Pond & waterways | muddy banks, reflections, lotus plants, monsoon runoff | UE Water shaders, decals and vegetation |
| Village market | tea stalls, Bangla signboards, fences, wires, benches | model props and ambient audio |
| Vehicles | bicycle, CNG auto, rickshaw, bus, local trucks | licensed meshes, physics, sounds and animations |
| People | rural clothing, accurate movements and Bangla interactions | permissioned models / voices, mocap |

First target: a single small pilot area centered on the village road, homes, rice fields and pond. Detailed 3D modelling and game profiling are essential before calling it realistic.

Only commit assets to a public repository when their licenses permit redistribution. Fab/Megascans assets should not be republished without the appropriate rights; use local Content imports and maintain a rights manifest.
